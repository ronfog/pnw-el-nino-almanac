Where the numbers come from

El Niño status and monthly leans: NOAA CPC ENSO discussion, Oct 8, 2026. Month-by-month leans: updated to CPC's Oct 15, 2026 seasonal outlook. Station checks: NWS monthly climate reports (CF6).

Weekly lines are tendencies from past strong El Niño winters (1997–98, 2015–16, 2023–24), not forecasts.

Planting dates

Vegetable, herb and annual dates (start indoors, set out transplants, sow outside) follow WSU Extension and OSU Extension calendars (EM 9027, EM 9473), shifted by zone and, for windows that open Jan–Mar only, by El Niño. Trees, shrubs and bulbs use rule-of-thumb windows. Check local frost dates before high-stakes plantings.
